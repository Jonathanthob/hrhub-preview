Who we are

An extension of your own team.

HR Hub Consultancy is a Johannesburg-based HR consultancy founded by Florence Malinga.

We work as an extension of your own team: we handle the policies, paperwork, employee relations and compliance that keep a business on the right side of South African labour law, so you can get on with running and growing yours.

We work with small, medium and growing businesses, on a once-off project or as ongoing support.
